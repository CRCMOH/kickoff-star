Privacy Policy for Kickoff Star

Last updated: August 2026

Kickoff Star ("the app") is a football career simulation game developed by Vraxis. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Kickoff Star does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The app uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads (for in-game bonuses such as energy, cash, or XP). To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • General device information (device type, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, for ad relevance)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can review Google's own data practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and their AdMob-specific policy at support.google.com/admob.

Game Save Data

Your game progress (career stats, team data, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to any server we control and is not accessible to us.

Ads and Advertising ID

You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising ID, through your device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on most Android devices).

Children's Privacy

Kickoff Star is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads served through AdMob are configured in accordance with Google's policies for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
